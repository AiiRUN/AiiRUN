# Product Vision

AiiRUN is not a clone of YMM4, Adobe Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, or CapCut. It synthesizes their strengths into one editing language.

Core positioning:

> 編集という行為そのものを、もっと統一的に設計し直したソフト

Strengths to synthesize:
- YMM4: intuitive object-centric editing, direct properties, text/subtitle ease, usable timeline.
- Premiere Pro: flexible timeline, keyframes, masks, nesting, shortcuts, extensibility, professional export.
- DaVinci Resolve: integrated video/audio/color/effects, GPU processing, professional color management.
- CapCut: fast results, templates, automation, AI assistance, beginner-friendly workflow.

Rule: "簡単" and "高機能" must not be treated as opposites.
